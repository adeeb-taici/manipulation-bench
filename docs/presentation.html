manipulation-bench

A unified multi-agent framework for measuring how AI systems manipulate, persuade, and reach consensus — across games, debates, networks, and social simulations.

Merging two existing frameworks + adding 9 new consensus game levels.

01

The problem

AI manipulation is becoming cheap,
scalable, and hard to measure

Frontier models exceed humans at persuasion. States already deploy AI disinformation campaigns. The EU AI Act prohibits manipulation but lacks measurement tools. The International AI Safety Report 2025 identifies manipulation as a major risk but notes the evidence base is insufficient for policy calibration.

Existing benchmarks (HarmBench, JailbreakBench) use binary metrics — attack succeeded or failed. They don't capture how far a system progresses through staged social engineering, or how group dynamics amplify or resist manipulation.

What's missing

  • Continuous manipulation depth (not binary pass/fail)
  • Multi-agent social dynamics (not single-turn prompts)
  • Consensus measurement (not just attack success)
  • Cross-environment comparison (debate vs. game vs. network)
  • Policy-calibrated metrics (EU AI Act, IAISR 2025)

Measurement system

Unified metrics across all environments

Scorer categories

Key insight

Scorers are organized by what they require, not by environment. Any level that tracks numeric opinions gets the full opinion suite. Any level with adaptive networks gets network metrics. No duplication.

Reproducibility

Most scorers are pure math — no LLM judge, fully reproducible. LLM-judge scorers (qualitative assessment) are clearly separated and labeled.

Summary

One framework to measure
how AI systems shape belief

manipulation-bench unifies game theory, social simulation, and opinion dynamics into a single evaluation framework. Built on Inspect AI. Every environment shares the same solver, scoring system, and analysis pipeline.

The key question at each level: does language change the dynamics of collective belief?

What makes this different

  • Continuous, not binary — measure manipulation depth
  • Multi-agent — social dynamics, not single prompts
  • Cross-environment — compare across 14 settings
  • Policy-ready — EU AI Act, IAISR 2025 aligned
  • Extensible — new environments in one file
